      Shooting steel safely

      • Keep pistols at least 10 m (11 yd) and rifles at least 100 m (110 yd) from steel: bullets break up and splash back.
      • Only shoot plates the round does not crater: a dent or crater throws splash straight back at the shooter.
      • Never shoot AP, steel-core or tungsten-core rounds at steel targets.
      • Hang plates so they swing, angled slightly down, and wear eye protection.
      The penetration model is an engineering estimate (cavity expansion for hard cores, Alekseevskii–Tate for eroding ones, a cratering law for bullets that splash), tuned to published figures. Don't stake anyone's safety on it.
